# Digital Synth PRA32-U2 Parameter Guide

- <https://github.com/risgk/digital-synth-pra32-u2>

## Main Parameters

- PRA32-U2 has many parameters, but a sound can be made mostly with the main parameters below: the first 32 in PRA32-U2 Editor (from Osc 1 Wave to EG/Amp Rel = Dec), and the Distortion Drive and the 2 FX levels
    - The other parameters can mostly be left as they are
    - "Randomize Synth" (Program Change #127) changes the first 32, except Voice Mode and Amp Gain
- To make a sound, we recommend starting from the Preset "#19 Plain Poly", rather than "#23 Initial"
    - "#19 Plain Poly" is a plain Saw Wave sound (Osc 1 Wave: Pulse Wave, with Osc 1 Morph: Saw 100%) in Poly, with the FX on (Chorus, and Reverb), while "#23 Initial" is in Mono, with the FX off
    - In "#19 Plain Poly", Osc 1 Morph changes the wave from the Saw: lower to a Pulse, higher to 2 Saws, with the pulse width (or the phase of the 2nd Saw) set by Osc 1 Shape (Osc 1 Shape has no effect while Osc 1 Morph is 64)
    - In both, EG Amp Mod is On: the EG also works as the Amp EG, so the Amp Attack/Decay/Sustain/Release are not used
- Voice and Pitch: Voice Mode, Portamento Time
- Osc: Osc 1 Wave, Mixer Noise/Sub Osc, Osc 1 Shape, Osc 1 Morph, Osc 2 Wave, Mixer Osc Mix, Osc 2 Coarse, Osc 2 Pitch
- Filter: Filter Cutoff, Filter Resonance, Filter EG Amt, Filter Key Track, Filter Mode, Filter Drive
- Amp: Amp Gain
- EG: EG Attack, EG Decay, EG Sustain, EG Release, EG Mod Amt, EG Mod Dst, EG Amp Mod, EG/Amp Rel = Dec
- LFO: LFO Wave, LFO Fade Time, LFO Rate, LFO Depth, LFO Mod Amt, LFO Mod Dst, LFO Filter Amt
- FX: Distortion Drive, Chorus Level, Delay Level

## Control Change Parameters

- Notes
    - $1 : Disabled if Osc 1 Wave is Tri (Triangle Wave)
- Osc 1 Wave [Saw|Sqr|Tri|Sin|WT|Pls]
    - 0 (0-12): Saw Wave
    - 26 (13-38): Square Wave
    - 51 (39-63): Triangle Wave
    - 77 (64-89): Sine Wave
    - 102 (90-115): Wave Tables with Constraints
    - 127 (116-127): Pulse Wave
- Mixer Noise/Sub Osc [N|S]
    - -64 (0): Noise 100% (min)
    - -62 (1-2): Noise 96.9%
    - -60 (3-4): Noise 93.8%
    - -2 (61-62): Noise 3.1%
    - +0 (63-64): 0%
    - +2 (65-66): Sub Osc 3.1%
    - +60 (123-124): Sub Osc 93.8%
    - +62 (125-126): Sub Osc 96.9%
    - +63 (127): Sub Osc 100% (max)
- Osc 1 Shape $1
    - Saw Wave: Multi Saw Detune
    - Square Wave: Sync Square Pitch
    - Sine Wave (Frequency Modulation or Phase Modulation): Modulation Depth
    - Wave Tables with Constraints: Position
    - Pulse Wave (= 1st Saw + Phase Shifted 2nd Saw)
        - 0: Pulse Width 50%, or 2nd Saw Phase 50%
        - 1: Pulse Width 49.6%, or 2nd Saw Phase 50.4%
        - 64: Pulse Width 25%, or 2nd Saw Phase 75%
        - 96: Pulse Width 12.5%, or 2nd Saw Phase 87.5%
        - 126: Pulse Width 0.8%, or 2nd Saw Phase 99.2%
        - 127: Pulse Width 0%, or 2nd Saw Phase 100%
- Osc 1 Morph $1
    - Saw Wave:
        - 0: Normal Saw 100% (min)
        - 64 (63-64): Normal Saw 50% + Multi Saw 50%
        - 127: Multi Saw 100% (max)
    - Square Wave:
        - 0: Normal Square 100% (min)
        - 64 (63-64): Normal Square 50% + Sync Square 50%
        - 127: Sync Square 100% (max)
    - Sine Wave (Frequency Modulation or Phase Modulation): Frequency Ratio of Modulator
        - 0 (0-1): Ratio 0.5 (min)
        - 4 (2-5): Ratio 1.0
        - 8 (6-9): Ratio 1.5
        - 12 (10-13): Ratio 2.0
        - 20 (18-21): Ratio 3.0
        - 28 (26-29): Ratio 4.0
        - 108 (106-109): Ratio 14.0
        - 124 (122-125): Ratio 16.0
        - 127 (126-127): Ratio 16.5 (max)
    - Wave Tables with Constraints
        - 0 (0-12): Wave Table 0 (Sync Pulse 1/8)
        - 26 (13-38): Wave Table 1 (Sync Pulse 1/4)
        - 51 (39-63): Wave Table 2 (Serial Sync 8/3)
        - 77 (64-89): Wave Table 3 (Sync & Ring)
        - 102 (90-115): Wave Table 4 (8+0 to 0+8)
        - 127 (116-127): Wave Table 5 (6+0 to 0+10)
    - Pulse Wave
        - 0: Pulse 100% = Saw 100% + Reverse Saw 100% (min)
        - 32 (31-32): Pulse 50% + Saw 50% = Saw 100% + Reverse Saw 50%
        - 64 (63-64): Saw 100%
        - 96 (95-96): Saw 100% + Saw 50%
        - 127: Saw 100% + Saw 100% (max)
        - NOTE: With a high "Osc 1 Shape" and a high "Osc 1 Morph", the output gets louder (up to about +6 dB), and the Output Compressor may make chords waver; if so, lower the "Amp Gain"
- Osc 2 Wave [Saw|Sqr|Tri|Sin|O1|Nos]
    - 0 (0-12): Saw Wave
    - 26 (13-38): Square Wave
    - 51 (39-63): Triangle Wave
    - 77 (64-89): Sine Wave
    - 102 (90-115): Same as Osc 1 Wave
        - Osc 1 Wave: Saw Wave -> Saw Wave
        - Osc 1 Wave: Square Wave -> Square Wave
        - Osc 1 Wave: Triangle Wave -> Triangle Wave
        - Osc 1 Wave: Sine Wave -> Sine Wave
        - Osc 1 Wave: Wave Tables with Constraints -> Square Wave
        - Osc 1 Wave: Pulse Wave -> Square Wave
    - 127 (116-127): White Noise
- Mixer Osc Mix [1|2]
    - 0: Osc 1 100% (min)
    - 64 (63-64): Osc 1 50% + Osc 2 50%
    - 127: Osc 2 100% (max)
- Osc 2 Coarse [-|+]

    - +60 (124-127): +60 semitone (max)
- Osc 2 Pitch [-|+]

    - -33 (31): -1 semitone
    - -32 (32): -50 cent

    - +1 (65): +1.5625 cent
    - +32 (96): +50 cent
    - +33 (97): +1 semitone
    - +45 (109): +7 semitone

- Filter Resonance
    - 0: Q = 0.707 (min)
    - 16: Q = 1.00
    - 32: Q = 1.41
    - 48: Q = 2.00
    - 64: Q = 2.83
    - 80: Q = 4.00
    - 96: Q = 5.66
    - 100: Q = 6.66
    - 104: Q = 9.15
    - 108: Q = 14.7
    - 112: Q = 27.4
    - 116: Q = 59.7
    - 120: Q = 152
    - 122: Q = 256
    - 123: Self-oscillation (peak level 0.15 out of the Filter; 0.22 in the loop)
    - 124: Self-oscillation (peak level 0.21 out of the Filter; 0.32 in the loop)
    - 125: Self-oscillation (peak level 0.25 out of the Filter; 0.39 in the loop)
    - 126: Self-oscillation (peak level 0.28 out of the Filter; 0.45 in the loop)
    - 127: Self-oscillation (peak level 0.30 out of the Filter; 0.50 in the loop) (max)
        - NOTE: The full scale is 1.0 (e.g. the peak level of the Osc 1 Saw Wave is 0.25)
    - Above 96, the output of the Filter is lowered with the Resonance (-1.3 dB at 108, -2.8 dB at 122, -3.5 dB at 123, -3.8 dB at 125, and -4.4 dB at 127), so that a high Resonance is about as loud as 96; how readily the self-oscillation holds against the input follows its level in the loop
    - The self-oscillation fades out as the cutoff (including the modulation) rises from the Cutoff 111 (f = 7.9 kHz) to 116 (f = 10.5 kHz), and above that the Resonance 123-127 acts as 122
    - A loud input near the cutoff (e.g. the Square or the Multi Saw) weakens or stops the self-oscillation, and an input harmonic near it pulls it to the pitch of that harmonic
    - NOTE: With a high Resonance (about 112 or above), chords can still get louder, and the Output Compressor lowers the level; if it is too much, lower the "Amp Gain"

- Amp Gain
    - NOTE: The Output Compressor (after the FX) compresses the peaks above -6 dB (0.5 of full scale) at its input by 2:1 (with a soft knee from -9 dB to -3 dB), limits the peaks so that they do not go above 1.0 (full scale) at its input (reached at +6 dB), and lowers the output by 2.5 dB (x 0.75) (with the Presets, the peaks of chords are lowered by about 0.5 dB)
- Filter Mode [LP|BR|HP|BP|LP]: The response of the Filter, changing smoothly in between (127 is taken as 128)
    - 0: Low Pass (min, the Presets)
    - 1-31 (L-R): Low Pass to Band Reject (the High Pass added: the notch comes down from the top to the cutoff)
    - 32: Band Reject (Notch)
    - 33-63 (R-H): Band Reject to High Pass (the Low Pass taken away: the notch goes on down and away)
    - 64: High Pass
    - 65-95 (H-B): High Pass to Band Pass (crossfaded; no notch in between)
    - 96: Band Pass
    - 97-126 (B-L): Band Pass to Low Pass (crossfaded; no notch in between)
    - 127: Low Pass (max, the same as 0)
- Filter Drive: The gain into the Filter, 2^(value / 24) (6 dB every 24 values; x 4 at 48, x 32 at 120, x 39.2 at 127), which drives the loop (the leak, at a high Resonance or near the cutoff) harder; what leaves the loop is lowered by the same ahead of the output clip, so that the level stays and the Drive changes the tone alone (the self-oscillation comes out lower by it, and a high Drive holds the resonant peak down; at a low Resonance it does little)
    - 0: x 1.00 (0 dB, min, the Presets: the Filter as it was)
    - 24: x 2.00 (+6 dB)
    - 48: x 4.00 (+12 dB)
    - 72: x 8.00 (+18 dB)
    - 96: x 16.0 (+24 dB)
    - 120: x 32.0 (+30 dB)
    - 127: x 39.2 (+31.9 dB, max)
- EG Amp Mod [Off|On]
    - 0 (0-63): Off, the Amp EG (Amp Attack/Decay/Sustain/Release, in the More Synth Parameters of PRA32-U2 Editor) changes the level (as a gate in the Presets: Amp Attack 0, Amp Decay 0, Amp Sustain 127, and Amp Release 0)
    - 127 (64-127): On, the EG also works as the Amp EG (the Amp Attack/Decay/Sustain/Release are not used)
- EG/Amp Rel = Dec [Off|On]
    - 0 (0-63): Off
    - 127 (64-127): On, EG Release = EG Decay and Amp Release = Amp Decay
- Osc/Filter Drift: Randomly drifts the pitch of each oscillator, and the Filter Cutoff of each voice by the same amount (100 cent = 1 Cutoff value), wandering very slowly (a time constant of about 44 s), as a slight difference of each oscillator that changes over a piece; it starts from a random value of the same spread (the same at every start, and goes back to it at All Sound Off, going the same way from there), not from 0
    - 0: Off
    - 32: About 1.4 cent (RMS) (the Presets)
    - 64: About 2.8 cent (RMS)
    - 127: About 5.6 cent (RMS) (max)
- Pitch Bend Range
    - 0: 0 semitone (min)
    - 64 (64-127): 64 semitone (max)
    - NOTE: Not changed by "Randomize Synth"
- Coarse Tune [-|+]
    - -64 (0): -64 semitone
    - -1 (63): -1 semitone
    - +0 (64): +0 semitone
    - +1 (65): +1 semitone
    - +63 (127): +63 semitone

- EG Att/Dec Velo Sens [-|+]: EG Attack/Decay Velocity Sensitivity, Modulates the attack/decay times by note on velocity
    - -64 (0): -100% (+63 to -63)
    - -32 (31-32): -50% (+31.5 to -31.5)
    - +0 (63-64): +0% (-0 to +0)
    - +32 (95-96): +50% (-31.5 to +31.5)
    - +63 (127): +100% (-63 to +63)
- EG Rel Velo Sens [-|+]: EG Release Velocity Sensitivity, Modulates the release time by note off velocity
    - -64 (0): -100% (+63 to -63)
    - -32 (31-32): -50% (+31.5 to -31.5)
    - +0 (63-64): +0% (-0 to +0)
    - +32 (95-96): +50% (-31.5 to +31.5)
    - +63 (127): +100% (-63 to +63)
- EG Level Velo Sens: EG Level Velocity Sensitivity, Decreases the EG level as note on velocity decreases
- Amp Level Velo Sens: Amp Level Velocity Sensitivity, Decreases the Amp EG level as note on velocity decreases
- EG Att/Dec Key Track [-|+]: Modulates the attack/decay times based on oscillator pitch at note-on
    - -64 (0): Delta Value = -12 / octave (Delta Second = 0.42x / octave)
    - -52 (11-12): Delta Value = -9.75 / octave (Delta Second = 0.50x / octave)
    - -32 (31-32): Delta Value = -6 / octave (Delta Second = 0.65x / octave)
    - +0 (63-64): Delta Value = +0 / octave (Delta Second = 1x / octave)
    - +32 (95-96): Delta Value = +6 / octave (Delta Second = 1.54x / octave)
    - +52 (115-116): Delta Value = +9.75 / octave (Delta Second = 2.02x / octave)
    - +63 (127): Delta Value = +12 / octave (Delta Second = 2.37x / octave)
- Voice Assign Mode [1|1|3|3|4|2]: The voice assignment in the Polyphonic Mode
    - 0 (0-12): Mode 1, Free voice with next number has priority (Round Robin), Release is effective
    - 26 (13-38): Mode 1, Free voice with next number has priority (Round Robin), Release is effective
    - 51 (39-63): Mode 3, Reuse note-off voices, Free voice with next number has priority (Round Robin), Release and polyphonic after touch are effective
    - 77 (64-89): Mode 3, Reuse note-off voices, Free voice with next number has priority (Round Robin), Release and polyphonic after touch are effective
    - 102 (90-115): Mode 4, Reuse note-off voices, Free voice with small number has priority, Portamento and polyphonic after touch are effective
    - 127 (116-127): Mode 2, Free voice with small number has priority, Portamento is effective
- Voice Group Mode [Off|Ly2|RLy|RR2|RR4|RR3] (PRA32-U2/M): Whether the Basic Channel + 1 to + 3 are used, and what + 4, + 5, and + 15 (the Layering) do (see PRA32-U2/M in README.md), by the value of the Main Synth (that of the Sub Synths is not used); the notes of the synths are turned off when it changes
    - 0 (0-12): Off, the Basic Channel alone, as PRA32-U2 (+ 1 to + 5 and + 15 not used; with PRA32-U2/M/P, the Panel Channel too)
    - 26 (13-38): Ly2, + 4 is the Layering of the synths 0 and 1, and + 5 that of 2 and 3
    - 51 (39-63): RLy, + 4 is the Round Robin (2 notes) of 0 and 1 and that of 2 and 3, layered
    - 77 (64-89): RR2, + 4 is the Round Robin (2 notes) of 0 and 1, and + 5 that of 2 and 3
    - 102 (90-115): RR4, + 4 is the Round Robin (4 notes) of 0, 1, 2, and 3
    - 127 (116-127): RR3, + 4 is the Round Robin (3 notes) of 1, 2, and 3 (the Presets)
    - NOTE: Not Off: the Basic Channel + 15 is the Layering of all too
    - NOTE: The Sub Synths sound only while the Main Synth is in the Mono modes: while it is in Poly, a group has only the Main Synth (none without it, e.g. RR3)
    - NOTE: Not changed by "Randomize Synth"; nothing in PRA32-U2 (not /M), nor without the Layering; not in PRA32-U2 Native
- LFO Trig Mode [Aut|Off|On|VOn|VOf|VAu]: How a note on restarts the LFO (its phase, and the LFO Fade Time), and whether the Voices share the LFO
    - 0 (0-12): Auto (Aut, the Presets), The LFO of all the Voices; the phase of Saw, S&H, and Square, and the fade, restart at the first note on (Polyphonic, Legato Portamento, and Legato) or every note on (Monophonic) (see Voice Mode and LFO Wave)
    - 26 (13-38): Off, The LFO of all the Voices, free running; the fade restarts as in Aut
    - 51 (39-63): On, The LFO of all the Voices; every note on restarts the phase (of every wave) and the fade
    - 77 (64-89): Voice On (VOn), An LFO for each Voice; the note on of the Voice restarts its phase (of every wave) and its fade
    - 102 (90-115): Voice Off (VOf), An LFO for each Voice, free running; the note on of the Voice restarts its fade
    - 127 (116-127): Voice Auto (VAu), An LFO for each Voice; the note on of the Voice restarts its phase of Saw, S&H, and Square, and its fade (Triangle and Sine run on)
    - NOTE: A restarted phase starts from the center going up (Triangle and Sine), or from the top (Saw and Square); S&H takes a new value
    - NOTE: The LFOs of the Voices start a quarter apart (at the start, and at All Sound Off), and the LFO of Voice N (1-4) runs a little faster, by about 0.0007 Hz x (N - 1), so that they drift apart over minutes; the Red Noise is shared. The 4 Synths of PRA32-U2/M are the same: Sub Synth N (1-3) starts at N / 4 and runs faster by about 0.0007 Hz x N, as Voice N + 1 of the Main Synth
    - NOTE: In the Monophonic modes (Voice Mode: Mon, LP, and Lgt), which play Voice 1, VOn, VOf, and VAu work as On, Off, and Aut
- Breath Filter Amt [-|+]: 2x the other Amt parameters (e.g. Filter EG Amt), so that a weak breath also opens the Filter
    - -60 (0-4): -240 (min)
    - +30 (94): +120, the Cutoff +120 with the max breath
    - +60 (124-127): +240 (max), the Cutoff +120 with the half breath
- Breath Amp Mode [Off|Q|L|LO|QO|Opn]
    - 0 (0-12): Off, The Amp follows the EG (the Breath Controller does not modulate the Amp)
    - 26 (13-38): Quadratic Curve (Q), The Amp follows the EG, x the Breath Controller (Quadratic Curve)
    - 51 (39-63): Linear Curve (L), The Amp follows the EG, x the Breath Controller (Linear Curve)
    - 77 (64-89): Linear Curve Open (LO), The Amp is held open (the EG is not used), x the Breath Controller (Linear Curve)
    - 102 (90-115): Quadratic Curve Open (QO), The Amp is held open (the EG is not used), x the Breath Controller (Quadratic Curve)
    - 127 (116-127): Open (Opn), The Amp is held open at the max (the EG is not used), and the level is controlled by the Filter (e.g. Breath Filter Amt)
    - NOTE: In the Open modes (LO, QO, Opn), the Amp EG, Amp Level Velo Sens, and the release are not effective, and the voices sound also after the note off (and before the first note on, at C4)
        - All Sound Off does not stop the sound in the Open modes (it works as All Notes Off, without resetting the Osc, the Filter, and the LFO)
        - With LO and QO, the sound stops when the Breath Controller is 0
        - With Opn, the level is controlled by the Low Pass Filter (Filter Mode: LP): close the Filter (a low Cutoff and a low Resonance) to stop the sound, and open it (by Cutoff, Breath Filter Amt, Filter EG Amt, or LFO Filter Amt) to sound
            - NOTE: Low notes may still be heard faintly at the min Cutoff
        - Opn can also be used for drones: the voices keep sounding at the last notes (in the Polyphonic Mode, the last chord of up to 4 notes), and the Filter EG and LFO still work
        - LO and QO are intended for the Monophonic or Legato Voice Mode, as the note-off voices keep sounding in the Polyphonic Mode
- After Touch LFO Amt: The LFO depth added by the max after touch (Polyphonic Key Pressure or Channel Pressure)
    - 0: +0% (min)
    - 64: +50%
    - 127: +100% (max)
- Portamento Mode [Sw|Aut]: What turns on and off the Portamento
    - 0 (0-63): Switch (Sw), The Portamento is on only while Portamento Switch (Control Change #65) is On (64-127)
    - 127 (64-127): Auto (Aut), The Portamento is always on
    - NOTE: In Legato Portamento (Voice Mode: LP), the Portamento is effective only for the legato notes, also while it is on
- Osc Saw Wave Mode [Str|Cur]
    - 0 (0-63): Straight Saw Wave
    - 127 (64-127): Curved Saw Wave, This does not affect the Pulse Wave
- Stretch Tune [-|+]
    - -64 (0): -37.5 cent at note number 108 (+37.5 cent at note number 12) (min)
    - -32 (32): -18.8 cent at note number 108 (+18.8 cent at note number 12)
    - +0 (64): +0 cent
    - +8 (72): +4.7 cent at note number 108 (-4.7 cent at note number 12) (the Presets)
    - +16 (80): +9.4 cent at note number 108 (-9.4 cent at note number 12)
    - +32 (96): +18.8 cent at note number 108 (-18.8 cent at note number 12)
    - +63 (127): +36.9 cent at note number 108 (-36.9 cent at note number 12) (max)
- Pan: Constant power panning
    - -64 (0-1): Left (L +3.0 dB, R Off) (min)
    - -32 (32): L +2.3 dB, R -5.5 dB
    - +0 (64): Center (L 0 dB, R 0 dB)
    - +32 (96): L -5.5 dB, R +2.3 dB
    - +63 (127): Right (L Off, R +3.0 dB) (max)
- Distortion Drive: The drive into the Distortion FX, 2^(value / 24) (6 dB every 24 values; x 4 at 48, x 32 at 120, x 39.2 at 127), for the synths whose FX Routing is Dst; the output is lowered by 15/16 of the drive in dB up to 96 (x 16), and by as much as there above it, where most of a sound is past the shoulder already, so that the level stays within about 2 dB as the drive goes up (a single quiet note gets a few dB louder at the top) (a little up halfway, and a little down where it saturates hard). The shoulder below zero is shorter than above (even harmonics too), the aliasing is reduced by ADAA (antiderivative anti-aliasing), and a tone (a 1-pole low pass) after it takes the edge off the harmonics, more with more drive (none at 0, about 10 kHz at 64, 5 kHz at 127)
    - NOTE: On a single note of the Saw Wave, which has every harmonic already, the Distortion changes the tone little; it is heard best on a chord (from a Distortion Drive of about 30 up, with the Presets), with a high Filter Resonance (about 110 or above, from a Distortion Drive of about 72 up), or with the Sine or the Triangle Wave
    - 0: x 1.00 (0 dB, min, the Presets: a sound below full scale passes as it is)
    - 24: x 2.00 (+6 dB)
    - 48: x 4.00 (+12 dB)
    - 72: x 8.00 (+18 dB)
    - 96: x 16.0 (+24 dB)
    - 120: x 32.0 (+30 dB)
    - 127: x 39.2 (+31.9 dB, max)
    - NOTE: Changed by "Randomize FX" (of PRA32-U2 Editor and PRA32-U2 Native), not by "Randomize Synth"
- FX Routing [Dst|Cho|Dly|Dly|Cmp|Dry]: Where the output is input to the FX (connected in series: Distortion -> Chorus -> Delay -> Output Compressor)
    - 0 (0-12): Dst (through the Distortion, the Chorus, the Delay, and the Output Compressor)
    - 26 (13-38): Chorus (through the Chorus, the Delay, and the Output Compressor, not the Distortion)
    - 51 (39-63): Delay (through the Delay and the Output Compressor)
    - 77 (64-89): Delay
    - 102 (90-115): Comp (through the Output Compressor only)
    - 127 (116-127): Dry (through none of them)
        - The output is still delayed by 1 ms and lowered by 2.5 dB (x 0.75) as the others, and is clipped (soft clipping) if it is too loud
    - In PRA32-U2/M, each synth has its own FX Routing, while the other FX parameters of the Main Synth apply to all synths
- Chorus Level: The level of the chorused signal L (0.0 at 0 to 1.0 at 127) added to the input; the sum is lowered by about 1 / sqrt(1 + L^2) (x 0.707 at 127, where the two are equal), so that the level stays within about 1 dB as the Chorus Level goes up
- Chorus Rate
    - 0: LFO Frequency 0.0114 Hz (min)
    - 64: LFO Frequency 0.482 Hz
    - 127: LFO Frequency 18.4 Hz (max)
- Chorus Depth
    - 0: Delay Time +/- 0 ms (min)
    - 32: Delay Time +/- 1.33 ms
    - 64: Delay Time +/- 2.67 ms
    - 126 (126-127): Delay Time +/- 5.25 ms (max)
- Delay Level: Delay Send Level
- Delay Mode [Ste|PP|Rev]
    - 0 (0-31): Stereo Delay
    - 64 (32-95): Ping Pong Delay
    - 127 (96-127): Reverb
        - The Delay Level, Delay Time, and Delay Feedback work as they do for the Delay: the same values give about the same length and loudness of the tail
        - Delay Time sets the size (the first reflection comes at about 1/3 of the Delay Time)
        - Delay Feedback sets the decay (the tail falls by the Delay Feedback over each 1.5 x Delay Time, so that it sounds about as long as the Delay's echoes)
        - When the Delay Time is changed, the size follows it slowly (within +/-3% of the pitch)
- Delay Time
    - 0: 1 ms (min)
    - 5: 6 ms
    - 6: 8 ms
    - 7: 10 ms
    - 12: 20 ms
    - 27: 50 ms
    - 42: 100 ms = eighth note time at 300 BPM
    - 57: 150 ms = eighth note time at 200 BPM
    - 62: 166.7 ms = eighth note time at 180 BPM
    - 63: 170 ms
    - 64: 173.3 ms
    - 72: 200 ms = eighth note time at 150 BPM
    - 87: 250 ms = eighth note time at 120 BPM
    - 93: 270 ms
    - 102: 300 ms = eighth note time at 100 BPM
    - 112: 333.3 ms
    - 114: 340 ms (max)
    - NOTE: When the Delay Time is changed, the delay time follows it within +/-25% of the pitch, as on a tape delay
- Delay Feedback
    - 0: Feedback 0% (min)
    - 64: Feedback 25%
    - 127: Feedback 49.6% (max)

## Performance Controllers

- Not stored in the programs; the values at startup and by Reset All Controllers are shown as "Reset"
- Pitch Bend: Bends the pitch by "Pitch Bend Range" (Reset: center)
- Modulation Wheel (Control Change #1): Adds to the LFO depth (127: +100%) (Reset: 0)
- Breath Controller (Control Change #2): Modulates the Filter Cutoff by "Breath Filter Amt", and the Amp by "Breath Amp Mode" (Reset: 0)
- Expression (Control Change #11): The level, in a quadratic curve (64: 25%, about -12 dB) (Reset: 127)
- Sustain Pedal [Off|On] (Control Change #64): While it is On (64-127), the note offs are held, and they are released when it is turned Off (Reset: Off)
    - It is also turned Off by All Notes Off, All Sound Off, Omni Mode Off/On, and Mono/Poly Mode On
- Portamento Switch [Off|On] (Control Change #65): While it is On (64-127), the Portamento is on, if "Portamento Mode" is Sw (Reset: Off)
- After Touch (Polyphonic Key Pressure and Channel Pressure): Adds to the LFO depth by "After Touch LFO Amt" (Reset: 0)

## Parameter Smoothing

- The parameters are smoothed, so that their steps (e.g. sent by MIDI controllers every 10 ms) are not heard
- The smoothing is updated at 6 kHz (every other control period of 12 kHz), for the CPU usage
- The EG and LFO modulations themselves are not smoothed (not delayed)
- 2 stages (5.3 ms average delay, 99% in 18 ms), which round off the corners of the steps, for the amounts and the balances below
- The amounts (of the tone, the level, or a modulation), whose steps are easily heard
    - Filter Cutoff, Filter Resonance, Filter EG Amt, LFO Filter Amt, Breath Filter Amt, Filter Drive, and the gains of the Filter Mode
    - Osc 1 Shape
    - EG Mod Amt and LFO Mod Amt, when EG Mod Dst and LFO Mod Dst are F (Filter Cutoff) or 1S (Osc 1 Shape)
    - Amp Gain
    - LFO Depth (with LFO Fade Time, Modulation Wheel, and After Touch)
    - Distortion Drive, Chorus Level, Chorus Depth, Delay Level, Delay Feedback
- The balances (between two sounds, or left and right), which keep the total amount about the same
    - Osc 1 Morph, Mixer Osc Mix, Mixer Noise/Sub Osc, Pan
    - For the Sine Wave and the Wave Tables, the smoothed Osc 1 Morph is rounded to the controller value, as it switches the ratio or the table step by step
- 1 stage (1.2 ms time constant, 95% in 3.7 ms), for the performance controllers, whose attack must not be softened, but whose steps must not click
    - Breath Controller (x Breath Filter Amt)
    - Expression x Breath Controller (Breath Amp Mode)
- 1 stage, for the Delay Time (5.3 ms), which moves in its own way
    - The Delay Time is also slew-limited, bending the pitch of the echoes (within +/-25%), as on a tape delay
- The smoothed parameters are calculated in fine steps (not in the controller value steps)
- Not smoothed: the pitch parameters, so that the pitch follows right away (Pitch Bend, and EG Mod Amt and LFO Mod Amt when EG Mod Dst and LFO Mod Dst are P or 2P), and the parameters whose steps are part of the sound (e.g. Osc 2 Coarse/Pitch, LFO Rate, EG times)
- See the comments in "pra32-u2-common.h" for details
