- 2026-10-26 ISGK Instruments
- <https://github.com/risgk/digital-synth-pra32-u2>

## Main Parameters

- PRA32-U2 has many parameters, but a sound can be made mostly with the main parameters below: the first 32 in PRA32-U2 Editor (from Osc 1 Wave to EG/Amp Rel = Dec), and the 2 FX levels
    - The other parameters can mostly be left as they are
    - "Randomize Synth" (Program Change #127) changes the first 32, except Voice Mode and Amp Gain
- To make a sound, we recommend starting from the Preset "#19 Plain Poly", rather than "#23 Initial"
    - "#19 Plain Poly" is a plain Saw Wave sound (Osc 1 Wave: Pulse Wave, with Osc 1 Morph: Saw 100%) in Poly, with the FX on (Chorus, and Reverb), while "#23 Initial" is in Mono, with the FX off
    - In "#19 Plain Poly", Osc 1 Morph changes the wave from the Saw: lower to a Pulse, higher to 2 Saws, with the pulse width (or the phase of the 2nd Saw) set by Osc 1 Shape (Osc 1 Shape has no effect while Osc 1 Morph is 64)
    - In both, EG Amp Mod is On: the EG also works as the Amp EG, so the Amp Attack/Decay/Sustain/Release are not used
- Voice and Pitch: Voice Mode, Portamento Time, Pitch Bend Range
- Osc: Osc 1 Wave, Mixer Noise/Sub Osc, Osc 1 Shape, Osc 1 Morph, Osc 2 Wave, Mixer Osc Mix, Osc 2 Coarse, Osc 2 Pitch
- Filter: Filter Cutoff, Filter Resonance, Filter EG Amt, Filter Key Track, Filter Mode
- Amp: Amp Gain
- EG: EG Attack, EG Decay, EG Sustain, EG Release, EG Mod Amt, EG Mod Dst, EG Amp Mod, EG/Amp Rel = Dec
- LFO: LFO Wave, LFO Fade Time, LFO Rate, LFO Depth, LFO Mod Amt, LFO Mod Dst, LFO Filter Amt
- FX: Chorus Level, Delay Level

    - 0: x 1.00 (0 dB, min, the Presets: the Filter as it was)
    - 32: x 2.00 (+6 dB)
    - 64: x 4.00 (+12 dB)
    - 96: x 8.00 (+18 dB)
    - 127: x 15.7 (+23.9 dB, max)
    - NOTE: Not changed by "Randomize Synth"

- Breath Amp Mode [Off|Q|L|LO|QO|Opn]
    - 0 (0-12): Off, The Amp follows the EG (the Breath Controller does not modulate the Amp)
    - 26 (13-38): Quadratic Curve (Q), The Amp follows the EG, x the Breath Controller (Quadratic Curve)
    - 51 (39-63): Linear Curve (L), The Amp follows the EG, x the Breath Controller (Linear Curve)
    - 77 (64-89): Linear Curve Open (LO), The Amp is held open (the EG is not used), x the Breath Controller (Linear Curve)
    - 102 (90-115): Quadratic Curve Open (QO), The Amp is held open (the EG is not used), x the Breath Controller (Quadratic Curve)
    - 127 (116-127): Open (Opn), The Amp is held open at the max (the EG is not used), and the level is controlled by the Filter (e.g. Breath Filter Amt)
    - NOTE: In the Open modes (LO, QO, Opn), the Amp EG, Amp Level Velo Sens, and the release are not effective, and the voices sound also after the note off (and before the first note on, at C4)
        - All Sound Off does not stop the sound in the Open modes (it works as All Notes Off, without resetting the Osc, the Filter, and the LFO)
        - With LO and QO, the sound stops when the Breath Controller is 0
        - With Opn, the level is controlled by the Low Pass Filter (Filter Mode: LP): close the Filter (a low Cutoff and a low Resonance) to stop the sound, and open it (by Cutoff, Breath Filter Amt, Filter EG Amt, or LFO Filter Amt) to sound
            - NOTE: Low notes may still be heard faintly at the min Cutoff
        - Opn can also be used for drones: the voices keep sounding at the last notes (in the Polyphonic Mode, the last chord of up to 4 notes), and the Filter EG and LFO still work
        - LO and QO are intended for the Monophonic or Legato Voice Mode, as the note-off voices keep sounding in the Polyphonic Mode
- After Touch LFO Amt: The LFO depth added by the max after touch (Polyphonic Key Pressure or Channel Pressure)
    - 0: +0% (min)
    - 64: +50%
    - 127: +100% (max)
- Portamento Mode [Sw|Aut]: What turns on and off the Portamento
    - 0 (0-63): Switch (Sw), The Portamento is on only while Portamento Switch (Control Change #65) is On (64-127)
    - 127 (64-127): Auto (Aut), The Portamento is always on
    - NOTE: In Legato Portamento (Voice Mode: LP), the Portamento is effective only for the legato notes, also while it is on
- Osc Saw Wave Mode [Str|Cur]
    - 0 (0-63): Straight Saw Wave
    - 127 (64-127): Curved Saw Wave, This does not affect the Pulse Wave
- Chorus Level: Chorus Send Level
- FX Routing [Cho|Cho|Dly|Dly|Cmp|Dry]: Where the output is input to the FX (connected in series: Chorus -> Delay -> Output Compressor)
    - 0 (0-12): Chorus (through the Chorus, the Delay, and the Output Compressor)
    - 26 (13-38): Chorus
    - 51 (39-63): Delay (through the Delay and the Output Compressor)
    - 77 (64-89): Delay
    - 102 (90-115): Comp (through the Output Compressor only)
    - 127 (116-127): Dry (through none of them)
        - The output is still delayed by 1 ms and lowered by 2.5 dB (x 0.75) as the others, and is clipped (soft clipping) if it is too loud
    - In PRA32-U2/M, each synth has its own FX Routing, while the other FX parameters of the Main Synth apply to all synths

- Chorus Depth
    - 0: Delay Time +/- 0 ms (min)
    - 32: Delay Time +/- 1.33 ms
    - 64: Delay Time +/- 2.67 ms
    - 126 (126-127): Delay Time +/- 5.25 ms (max)
- Delay Level: Delay Send Level
- Delay Mode [Ste|PP|Rev]
    - 0 (0-31): Stereo Delay
    - 64 (32-95): Ping Pong Delay
    - 127 (96-127): Reverb
        - The Delay Level, Delay Time, and Delay Feedback work as they do for the Delay: the same values give about the same length and loudness of the tail
        - Delay Time sets the size (the first reflection comes at about 1/3 of the Delay Time)
        - Delay Feedback sets the decay (the tail falls by the Delay Feedback over each 1.5 x Delay Time, so that it sounds about as long as the Delay's echoes)
        - When the Delay Time is changed, the size follows it slowly (within +/-3% of the pitch)

    - 127: Feedback 49.6% (max)

## Performance Controllers

- Not stored in the programs; the values at startup and by Reset All Controllers are shown as "Reset"
- Pitch Bend: Bends the pitch by "Pitch Bend Range" (Reset: center)
- Modulation Wheel (Control Change #1): Adds to the LFO depth (127: +100%) (Reset: 0)
- Breath Controller (Control Change #2): Modulates the Filter Cutoff by "Breath Filter Amt", and the Amp by "Breath Amp Mode" (Reset: 0)
- Expression (Control Change #11): The level, in a quadratic curve (64: 25%, about -12 dB) (Reset: 127)
- Sustain Pedal [Off|On] (Control Change #64): While it is On (64-127), the note offs are held, and they are released when it is turned Off (Reset: Off)
    - It is also turned Off by All Notes Off, All Sound Off, Omni Mode Off/On, and Mono/Poly Mode On
- Portamento Switch [Off|On] (Control Change #65): While it is On (64-127), the Portamento is on, if "Portamento Mode" is Sw (Reset: Off)
- After Touch (Polyphonic Key Pressure and Channel Pressure): Adds to the LFO depth by "After Touch LFO Amt" (Reset: 0)

## Parameter Smoothing

- The parameters are smoothed, so that their steps (e.g. sent by MIDI controllers every 10 ms) are not heard
- The smoothing is updated at 6 kHz (every other control period of 12 kHz), for the CPU usage
- The EG and LFO modulations themselves are not smoothed (not delayed)
- 2 stages (5.3 ms average delay, 99% in 18 ms), which round off the corners of the steps, for the amounts and the balances below
- The amounts (of the tone, the level, or a modulation), whose steps are easily heard
    - Filter Cutoff, Filter Resonance, Filter EG Amt, LFO Filter Amt, Breath Filter Amt
    - Osc 1 Shape
    - EG Mod Amt and LFO Mod Amt, when EG Mod Dst and LFO Mod Dst are F (Filter Cutoff) or 1S (Osc 1 Shape)
    - Amp Gain
    - LFO Depth (with LFO Fade Time, Modulation Wheel, and After Touch)
    - Chorus Level, Chorus Depth, Delay Level, Delay Feedback
- The balances (between two sounds, or left and right), which keep the total amount about the same
    - Osc 1 Morph, Mixer Osc Mix, Mixer Noise/Sub Osc, Pan
    - For the Sine Wave and the Wave Tables, the smoothed Osc 1 Morph is rounded to the controller value, as it switches the ratio or the table step by step
- 1 stage (1.2 ms time constant, 95% in 3.7 ms), for the performance controllers, whose attack must not be softened, but whose steps must not click
    - Breath Controller (x Breath Filter Amt)
    - Expression x Breath Controller (Breath Amp Mode)
- 1 stage, for the Delay Time (5.3 ms), which moves in its own way
    - The Delay Time is also slew-limited, bending the pitch of the echoes (within +/-25%), as on a tape delay
- The smoothed parameters are calculated in fine steps (not in the controller value steps)
- Not smoothed: the pitch parameters, so that the pitch follows right away (Pitch Bend, and EG Mod Amt and LFO Mod Amt when EG Mod Dst and LFO Mod Dst are P or 2P), and the parameters whose steps are part of the sound (e.g. Osc 2 Coarse/Pitch, LFO Rate, EG times)
- See the comments in "pra32-u2-common.h" for details
