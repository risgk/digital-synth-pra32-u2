# Digital Synth PRA32-U2 Parameter Guide

- <https://github.com/risgk/digital-synth-pra32-u2>

## Main Parameters

- PRA32-U2 has many parameters, but a sound can be made mostly with the main parameters below: the first 32 in PRA32-U2 Editor (from Osc 1 Wave to EG/Amp Rel = Dec), and the Distortion Drive and the 2 FX levels
    - The other parameters can mostly be left as they are
    - "Randomize Synth" (Program Change #127) changes the first 32, except Voice Mode and Amp Gain
- Voice and Pitch: Voice Mode, Portamento Time
- Osc: Osc 1 Wave, Mixer Noise/Sub Osc, Osc 1 Shape, Osc 1 Morph, Osc 2 Wave, Mixer Osc Mix, Osc 2 Coarse, Osc 2 Pitch
- Filter: Filter Cutoff, Filter Resonance, Filter EG Amt, Filter Key Track, Filter Mode, Filter Drive
- Amp: Amp Gain
- EG: EG Attack, EG Decay, EG Sustain, EG Release, EG Mod Amt, EG Mod Dst, EG Amp Mod, EG/Amp Rel = Dec
- LFO: LFO Wave, LFO Fade Time, LFO Rate, LFO Depth, LFO Mod Amt, LFO Mod Dst, LFO Filter Amt
- FX: Distortion Drive, Chorus Level, Delay Level

## Values Shown

- PRA32-U2 Editor, PRA32-U2 Native, and the panel of PRA32-U2/P show the values of many parameters as they work, in 3 characters at most
  (beside the controller value; "Shown" below), the same in all three
    - Times: the milliseconds without the unit (e.g. "100" for 100 ms), and the seconds with "." (e.g. "1.0" for 1.00 s, and "28." for 27.9 s)
    - Frequencies, and the other values of this form (e.g. the Q): ".01" to ".99" below 1, "1.0" to "9.9", "10" to "999", and "1k0" to "99k" (kHz)
    - %: with "%" where it fits (e.g. "2%", "50%"), and "100" for 100%
    - dB, cents, and semitones: the numbers, without the unit
    - The sign: shown (e.g. "+2", "-98"), but where it does not fit (e.g. "110" for +110 and -110, and "100" for -100 cents)
- The values are rounded; a value other than 0 is not shown as 0 (e.g. Filter Drive "+1" for +0.25 dB), and Delay Feedback 49.6% is "49%"
- Where 127 is taken as 128 (e.g. Osc 1 Shape), it is shown as 128
- The values are of the parameters themselves, without the modulations (e.g. Filter Cutoff without the EG, the LFO, and Filter Key Track)

## Control Change Parameters

- Osc 1 Wave [Saw|Sqr|Tri|Sin|WT|Pls]
    - 0 (0-12): Saw Wave
    - 26 (13-38): Square Wave
    - 51 (39-63): Triangle Wave
    - 77 (64-89): Sine Wave
    - 102 (90-115): Wave Tables with Constraints
    - 127 (116-127): Pulse Wave
- Mixer Noise/Sub Osc [N to S]: 2 values a step (3.1%)
    - -64 (0): Noise 100% (min)
    - +0 (63-64): 0%
    - +63 (127): Sub Osc 100% (max)
    - Shown: "N64", "N62" (1-2), ..., "0", ..., "S64"
- Osc 1 Shape (127 is taken as 128)
    - Saw Wave: Multi Saw Detune (7 saws), changing smoothly with the Shape (and its EG and LFO modulations)
        - Shown: the detune of the outer 2 saws in cents (e.g. "2.4" at 0, "41" at 64, and "79" at 127)
    - Square Wave: Sync Square Pitch, the frequency ratio 2^(value / 32) / 2 (an octave every 32 values)
        - Shown: the ratio (e.g. ".50" at 0, "1.0" at 32, and "8.0" at 127)
    - Triangle Wave: Fold Gain (a wavefolder: the wave beyond ±1 folded back), x1 (0) to x9 (127), +1 every 16 values
        - At an odd gain (32, 64, 96, and 127), the folded wave is the Triangle Wave of the gain times the pitch
        - Lowered at a high pitch, so that the gain times the frequency stays within 12 kHz (e.g. x5.7 at C7)
        - Shown: the gain, not as lowered (e.g. "1.0" at 0, "5.0" at 64, and "9.0" at 127)
    - Sine Wave (Frequency Modulation or Phase Modulation): Modulation Depth, up to 4 periods of the phase at 127
        - Shown: the depth in periods (e.g. ".50" at 16, "2.0" at 64, and "4.0" at 127)
    - Wave Tables with Constraints: Position
        - Shown: 0-126, and "128" at 127
    - Pulse Wave (= 1st Saw + Phase Shifted 2nd Saw): Pulse Width, or 2nd Saw Phase, value / 128
        - 0: 0% (min)
        - 64: 50%
        - 127: 100% (max)
        - Shown: the pulse width (e.g. "50%" at 64, and "100" at 127)
        - The EG and LFO modulations stay within the range of the Shape (0-127), as with the other waves
        - NOTE: The Pulse Width 0% and 100% are silent (with the Pulse), as the 2nd Saw cancels the 1st:
          set Osc 1 Shape to 64 (50%) when Osc 1 Wave is changed to Pls with Osc 1 Shape 0
- Osc 1 Morph
    - Saw Wave, Square Wave, and Triangle Wave: the mix of the Normal and the other wave, 2 values a step
        - 0: Normal Saw, Normal Square, or Normal Triangle 100% (min)
        - 64 (63-64): 50% + 50%
        - 127: Multi Saw, Sync Square, or Folded Triangle 100% (max)
        - Shown: the Multi Saw, the Sync Square, or the Folded Triangle in % (e.g. "50%" at 63-64)
    - Sine Wave (Frequency Modulation or Phase Modulation): Frequency Ratio of Modulator, 0.5 (0-1) to 16.5 (126-127), +0.5 every 4 values
        - e.g. 1.0 at 4 (2-5), 2.0 at 12 (10-13), and 4.0 at 28 (26-29)
        - Shown: the ratio, with "." for .5 (e.g. "1.5", and "16." for 16.5)
    - Wave Tables with Constraints
        - 0 (0-12): Wave Table 0 (Sync Pulse 1/8)
        - 26 (13-38): Wave Table 1 (Sync Pulse 1/4)
        - 51 (39-63): Wave Table 2 (Serial Sync 8/3)
        - 77 (64-89): Wave Table 3 (Sync & Ring)
        - 102 (90-115): Wave Table 4 (8+0 to 0+8)
        - 127 (116-127): Wave Table 5 (6+0 to 0+10)
        - Shown: "WT0" to "WT5"; the slider of PRA32-U2 Editor snaps to the 6 tables
    - Pulse Wave: 2 values a step
        - 0: Pulse 100% = Saw 100% + Reverse Saw 100% (min)
        - 32 (31-32): Pulse 50% + Saw 50% = Saw 100% + Reverse Saw 50%
        - 64 (63-64): Saw 100%
        - 96 (95-96): Saw 100% + Saw 50%
        - 127: Saw 100% + Saw 100% (max)
        - Shown: "P64" (the Pulse), ..., "0" (the Saw), ..., "S64" (the 2nd Saw)
        - NOTE: With "Osc 1 Shape" near 0 or 127 and a high "Osc 1 Morph", the output gets louder (up to about +6 dB), and the Output Compressor may make chords waver; if so, lower the "Amp Gain"
- Osc 2 Wave [Saw|Sqr|Tri|Sin|O1|Nos]
    - 0 (0-12): Saw Wave
    - 26 (13-38): Square Wave
    - 51 (39-63): Triangle Wave
    - 77 (64-89): Sine Wave
    - 102 (90-115): Same as Osc 1 Wave (Square Wave for Wave Tables with Constraints and Pulse Wave)
    - 127 (116-127): White Noise
- Mixer Osc Mix [1 to 2]: The power of the two, 2 values a step
    - 0: Osc 1 100% (min)
    - 64 (63-64): Osc 1 50% + Osc 2 50%
    - 127: Osc 2 100% (max)
    - Shown: Osc 2 in % (e.g. "50%" at 63-64)
- Osc 2 Coarse [- to +]: -60 (0-4) to +60 (124-127) semitone
- Osc 2 Pitch [- to +]
    - -32 (32) to +32 (96): -50 to +50 cent, 1.5625 cent a value
    - -55 (9) to -33 (31), +33 (97) to +55 (119): -12 to -1, +1 to +12 semitone, 0.5 semitone a value (min and max)
    - Shown: the cents (e.g. "+2" at +1), and the semitones, with "." for .5 (e.g. "+1." at +34, and "10." for +10.5 and -10.5)
- Filter Cutoff: 13.75 Hz x 2^((value - 1) / 12), a semitone a value
    - 0: 13.0 Hz (min)
    - 61: 440.0 Hz
    - 127: 19.9 kHz (max)
    - Shown: the frequency (e.g. "440", "3k5", and "20k")
- Filter Resonance
    - 0: Q = 0.707 (min)
    - 16-96: Q = 1.00 to 5.66, doubling every 32 values
    - 108: Q = 14.7
    - 122: Q = 256
    - 123-127: Self-oscillation (peak level 0.15 to 0.30 out of the Filter, 0.22 to 0.50 in the loop; the full scale is 1.0, and the Osc 1 Saw Wave is 0.25)
    - Above 96, the output of the Filter is lowered with the Resonance (-1.3 dB at 108, -2.8 dB at 122, and -4.4 dB at 127), so that a high Resonance is about as loud as 96
    - The self-oscillation fades out as the cutoff (including the modulation) rises from the Cutoff 111 (7.9 kHz) to 116 (10.5 kHz), and above that the Resonance 123-127 acts as 122
    - A loud input near the cutoff (e.g. the Square or the Multi Saw) weakens or stops the self-oscillation, and an input harmonic near it pulls it to the pitch of that harmonic
    - Shown: the Q (e.g. ".71", "5.7", and "256"), and "Osc" for 123-127
    - NOTE: With a high Resonance (about 112 or above), chords can still get louder, and the Output Compressor lowers the level; if it is too much, lower the "Amp Gain"
- Filter EG Amt [- to +], LFO Filter Amt [- to +]: The Cutoff at the full EG or LFO, 2 a value
    - -60 (0-4): -120 (min)
    - +60 (124-127): +120 (max)
    - Shown: the Cutoff (the semitones) (e.g. "+2", "-98", and "110")
- Filter Key Track [- to +]: How much the Cutoff follows the pitch, 2 values a step (3.125%)
    - 0: -100% (min)
    - 64 (63-64): 0%
    - 96 (95-96): +50%
    - 127: +100% (max)
    - Shown: the % (e.g. "+3%", "+13", and "100")
- EG Attack, Amp Attack: Attack time, 1 ms x 10^(value / 32)
    - 0: 1.00 ms
    - 64: 100 ms
    - 127: 9.31 s
- EG Decay, EG Release, Amp Decay, Amp Release: Time to 1/1024 (approx. -60 dB), 3 times the Attack
    - 0: 3.00 ms
    - 64: 300 ms
    - 127: 27.9 s
- EG Attack, EG Decay, EG Release, Amp Attack, Amp Decay, and Amp Release: Shown: the time (e.g. "100", "1.0", and "28.")
- EG Sustain, Amp Sustain: Sustain level, 2 values a step
    - 0: 0%
    - 64 (63-64): 50%
    - 127: 100%
    - Shown: the % (e.g. "2%" at 1-2, and "100")
- EG Mod Amt [- to +], LFO Mod Amt [- to +]: By EG Mod Dst and LFO Mod Dst
    - Pitch (P and 2P), at the full EG or LFO
        - -32 (32) to +32 (96): -100 to +100 cent, 3.125 cent a value
        - -33 (31) to -55 (9), +33 (97) to +55 (119): -2 to -24, +2 to +24 semitone, 1 semitone a value
        - -56 (8) to -63 (1), +56 (120) to +63 (127): -2.5 to -10, +2.5 to +10 oct (min and max)
        - Shown: the cents (e.g. "+3" at +1), and the semitones (e.g. "+1" at +32, and "120" at +63 and -63)
    - Shape (1S), 2 a value
        - -63 (1): Shape -128 (min)
        - +0 (64): Shape +0
        - +63 (127): Shape +128 (max)
        - At the full EG; the LFO swings the Shape by half of it on each side (e.g. -64 to +64 at +63, the pulse width 0% to 100% around 64 with the Pulse Wave)
        - Shown: the Shape at the full EG (e.g. "+2", and "128")
    - Cutoff (F): as Filter EG Amt
        - Shown: as Filter EG Amt
- EG Mod Dst [P|P|2P|2P|F|1S], LFO Mod Dst [P|P|2P|2P|F|1S]
    - 0 (0-12): Osc 1 & 2 Pitch
    - 26 (13-38): Osc 1 & 2 Pitch
    - 51 (39-63): Osc 2 Pitch
    - 77 (64-89): Osc 2 Pitch
    - 102 (90-115): Filter Cutoff
    - 127 (116-127): Osc 1 Shape
- Voice Mode [Pol|Pol|Mon|Mon|LP|Lgt]
    - 0 (0-12): Polyphonic (LFO Single Trigger)
    - 26 (13-38): Polyphonic (LFO Single Trigger)
    - 51 (39-63): Monophonic (EG & LFO Multi Trigger)
    - 77 (64-89): Monophonic (EG & LFO Multi Trigger)
    - 102 (90-115): Legato Portamento (Monophonic, EG & LFO Single Trigger, Auto Portamento)
    - 127 (116-127): Legato (Monophonic, EG & LFO Single Trigger)
    - NOTE: The LFO triggers as above with "LFO Trig Mode" Aut; see it for the others
- Portamento Time: as EG Attack (0: none)
    - 0: 0 ms
    - 64: 100 ms
    - 127: 9.31 s
    - Shown: the time (e.g. "0", "100", and "9.3")
    - With Portamento Mode ESw and EAu (Exponential): the time that the pitch takes to approach the note (exponentially)
    - With Portamento Mode LSw and LAu (Linear): the time of an octave, at a constant rate (e.g. at 64, 12 cents/ms: an octave
      in 100 ms, a semitone in 8.3 ms, and two octaves in 200 ms), so that a wider interval takes longer, as Portamento Time of GM2
      (General MIDI Level 2) sets a rate
    - NOTE: With the same value, an octave takes about the same time in both, and a narrower interval less time with the linear one
      (e.g. at 64, to within 1 cent: a semitone in 66 ms, and in 8.3 ms with the linear one), a wider one more
    - NOTE: The Portamento is turned on and off by "Portamento Mode" (and "Portamento Switch")
- LFO Wave [Tri|Sin|Nos|Saw|S&H|Sqr]
    - 0 (0-12): Triangle Wave (Key Trigger Off, -0.5 to +0.5)
    - 26 (13-38): Sine Wave (Key Trigger Off, -0.5 to +0.5)
    - 51 (39-63): Red Noise (Key Trigger Off, -0.5 to +0.5)
    - 77 (64-89): Saw Wave (Key Trigger On, -0.5 to +0.5)
    - 102 (90-115): Sample & Hold (Key Trigger On, -0.5 to +0.5)
    - 127 (116-127): Square Wave (Key Trigger On, 0.0 to 1.0)
    - NOTE: Key Trigger On/Off is with "LFO Trig Mode" Aut and VAu; with On and VOn, every wave is key triggered
- LFO Fade Time
    - 0: 0 ms (min)
    - 64: 992 ms
    - 127: 9.64 s (max)
    - Shown: the time (e.g. "992", and "9.6")
- LFO Rate
    - 0: 0 Hz (min)
    - 64: 2.73 Hz
    - 79: 6.49 Hz
    - 127: 104 Hz (max)
    - Shown: the frequency (e.g. ".07" at 1, "2.7", and "104")
    - With Red Noise: sets the cutoff of the low pass of the noise (the level stays the same)
        - 1: 0.0146 Hz
        - 64: 189 Hz
        - 83: 5.12 kHz
        - 84-127: No Low-Pass Filtering, about (max at 100-127)
        - Shown: the cutoff (-3 dB) (e.g. "189", and "5k1"), and "Off" for 84-127
- LFO Depth
    - 0: 0% (min)
    - 64: 50%
    - 127: 100% (max)
    - Shown: the % (e.g. "1%" at 1, and "100")
    - NOTE: The actual LFO depth is the "LFO Depth" value plus the "Modulation Wheel" value and the after touch effect (up to 100%)
- Amp Gain: The level, (value / 127)^2
    - Shown: the gain in dB (e.g. "0" at 127, "-1" at 117-126, "-12" at 64, and "-84" at 1), and "Off" at 0
        - By the gain as it is calculated (in 1/16384, the integer), e.g. "-43" at 11 (-42.6 dB), where (11 / 127)^2 is -42.5 dB
    - NOTE: The Output Compressor (after the FX) compresses the peaks above -6 dB (0.5 of full scale) at its input by 2:1 (with a soft knee from -9 dB to -3 dB), limits the peaks so that they do not go above 1.0 (full scale) at its input (reached at +6 dB), and lowers the output by 2.5 dB (x 0.75) (with the Presets, the peaks of chords are lowered by about 0.5 dB)
- Filter Mode [LP-BR-HP-BP-LP]: The response of the Filter, changing smoothly in between (127 is taken as 128)
    - 0: Low Pass (min)
    - 1-31 (L-R): Low Pass to Band Reject (the High Pass added: the notch comes down from the top to the cutoff)
    - 32: Band Reject (Notch)
    - 33-63 (R-H): Band Reject to High Pass (the Low Pass taken away: the notch goes on down and away)
    - 64: High Pass
    - 65-95 (H-B): High Pass to Band Pass (crossfaded; no notch in between)
    - 96: Band Pass
    - 97-126 (B-L): Band Pass to Low Pass (crossfaded; no notch in between)
    - 127: Low Pass (max, the same as 0)
- Filter Drive: The gain into the Filter, 2^(value / 24) (6 dB every 24 values), which drives the loop (the leak, at a high Resonance or near the cutoff) harder; what leaves the loop is lowered by the same ahead of the output clip, so that the level stays and the Drive changes the tone alone (the self-oscillation comes out lower by it, and a high Drive holds the resonant peak down; at a low Resonance it does little)
    - 0: x 1.00 (0 dB, min: the Filter without the Drive)
    - 48: x 4.00 (+12 dB)
    - 120: x 32.0 (+30 dB)
    - 127: x 39.2 (+31.9 dB, max)
    - Shown: the dB (e.g. "+6", and "+32")
- EG Amp Mod [Off|On]
    - 0 (0-63): Off, the Amp EG (Amp Attack/Decay/Sustain/Release, in the More Synth Parameters of PRA32-U2 Editor) changes the level (e.g. as a gate: Amp Attack 0, Amp Decay 0, Amp Sustain 127, and Amp Release 0)
    - 127 (64-127): On, the EG also works as the Amp EG (the Amp Attack/Decay/Sustain/Release are not used)
- EG/Amp Rel = Dec [Off|On]
    - 0 (0-63): Off
    - 127 (64-127): On, EG Release = EG Decay and Amp Release = Amp Decay
- Osc/Filter Drift: Randomly drifts the pitch of each oscillator, and the Filter Cutoff of each voice by the same amount (100 cent = 1 Cutoff value), wandering very slowly (a time constant of about 44 s), as a slight difference of each oscillator that changes over a piece; it starts from a random value of the same spread (the same at every start, and goes back to it at All Sound Off, going the same way from there), not from 0
    - 0: Off
    - 32: About 1.4 cent (RMS)
    - 127: About 5.6 cent (RMS) (max)
- Pitch Bend Range: 0 (min) to 64 (64-127, max) semitone
    - Shown: the semitones (e.g. "64" for 64-127)
    - NOTE: Not changed by "Randomize Synth"
- Coarse Tune [- to +]: -64 (0) to +63 (127) semitone
- Fine Tune [- to +]: -100 (0) to +98.4375 (127) cent, 1.5625 cent a value
    - Shown: the cents (e.g. "+2" at +1, and "100" at -64)
- EG Att/Dec Velo Sens [- to +]: EG Attack/Decay Velocity Sensitivity, Modulates the attack/decay times by note on velocity, 2 values a step (3.125%)
    - -64 (0): -100% (+63 to -63 by the velocity, in the values of the times)
    - +0 (63-64): +0%
    - +63 (127): +100% (-63 to +63)
    - Shown: the % (e.g. "+3%", "+50", and "100")
- EG Rel Velo Sens [- to +]: EG Release Velocity Sensitivity, Modulates the release time by note off velocity, as EG Att/Dec Velo Sens
    - Shown: as EG Att/Dec Velo Sens
- EG Level Velo Sens: EG Level Velocity Sensitivity, Decreases the EG level as note on velocity decreases, 2 values a step
    - 0: 0% (min, the EG level does not change by the velocity)
    - 64 (63-64): 50%
    - 127: 100% (max, the EG level in proportion to the velocity)
    - Shown: the % (e.g. "50%")
- Amp Level Velo Sens: Amp Level Velocity Sensitivity, Decreases the Amp EG level as note on velocity decreases, as EG Level Velo Sens
    - Shown: as EG Level Velo Sens
- EG Att/Dec Key Track [- to +]: Modulates the attack/decay times based on oscillator pitch at note-on, 2 values a step
    - -64 (0): Delta Value = -12 / octave (Delta Second = 0.42x / octave)
    - +0 (63-64): Delta Value = +0 / octave (Delta Second = 1x / octave)
    - +63 (127): Delta Value = +12 / octave (Delta Second = 2.37x / octave)
    - Shown: the % of +-12 / octave (e.g. "+50", and "100")
- Voice Assign Mode [1|1|3|3|4|2]: The voice assignment in the Polyphonic Mode
    - 0 (0-12): Mode 1, Free voice with next number has priority (Round Robin), Release is effective
    - 26 (13-38): Mode 1, Free voice with next number has priority (Round Robin), Release is effective
    - 51 (39-63): Mode 3, Reuse note-off voices, Free voice with next number has priority (Round Robin), Release and polyphonic after touch are effective
    - 77 (64-89): Mode 3, Reuse note-off voices, Free voice with next number has priority (Round Robin), Release and polyphonic after touch are effective
    - 102 (90-115): Mode 4, Reuse note-off voices, Free voice with small number has priority, Portamento and polyphonic after touch are effective
    - 127 (116-127): Mode 2, Free voice with small number has priority, Portamento is effective
- Voice Group Mode [Off|Ly2|RLy|RR2|RR4|RR3] (PRA32-U2/M): Whether the Basic Channel + 1 to + 3 are used, and what + 4, + 5, and + 15 (the Layering) do (see PRA32-U2/M in README.md), by the value of the Main Synth (that of the Sub Synths is not used); the notes of the synths are turned off when it changes
    - 0 (0-12): Off, the Basic Channel alone, as PRA32-U2 (+ 1 to + 5 and + 15 not used; with PRA32-U2/M/P, the Panel Channel too)
    - 26 (13-38): Ly2, + 4 is the Layering of the synths 0 and 1, and + 5 that of 2 and 3
    - 51 (39-63): RLy, + 4 is the Round Robin (2 notes) of 0 and 1 and that of 2 and 3, layered
    - 77 (64-89): RR2, + 4 is the Round Robin (2 notes) of 0 and 1, and + 5 that of 2 and 3
    - 102 (90-115): RR4, + 4 is the Round Robin (4 notes) of 0, 1, 2, and 3
    - 127 (116-127): RR3, + 4 is the Round Robin (3 notes) of 1, 2, and 3
    - NOTE: Not Off: the Basic Channel + 15 is the Layering of all too
    - NOTE: The Sub Synths sound only while the Main Synth is in the Mono modes: while it is in Poly, the notes of a group go only to the Main Synth (none without it, e.g. RR3), and the other messages (e.g. Control Changes) to all of the group
    - NOTE: Not changed by "Randomize Synth"; nothing in PRA32-U2 (not /M), nor without the Layering; not in PRA32-U2 Native
- LFO Trig Mode [Aut|Off|On|VOn|VOf|VAu]: How a note on restarts the LFO (its phase, and the LFO Fade Time), and whether the Voices share the LFO
    - 0 (0-12): Auto (Aut), The LFO of all the Voices; the phase of Saw, S&H, and Square, and the fade, restart at the first note on (Polyphonic, Legato Portamento, and Legato) or every note on (Monophonic) (see Voice Mode and LFO Wave)
    - 26 (13-38): Off, The LFO of all the Voices, free running; the fade restarts as in Aut
    - 51 (39-63): On, The LFO of all the Voices; the phase (of every wave) and the fade restart at the first note on (Polyphonic, Legato Portamento, and Legato) or every note on (Monophonic), as Aut
    - 77 (64-89): Voice On (VOn), An LFO for each Voice; the note on of the Voice restarts its phase (of every wave) and its fade
    - 102 (90-115): Voice Off (VOf), An LFO for each Voice, free running; the note on of the Voice restarts its fade
    - 127 (116-127): Voice Auto (VAu), An LFO for each Voice; the note on of the Voice restarts its phase of Saw, S&H, and Square, and its fade (Triangle and Sine run on)
    - NOTE: A restarted phase starts from the center going up (Triangle and Sine), or from the top (Saw and Square); S&H takes a new value
    - NOTE: The LFOs of the Voices start a quarter apart (at the start, and at All Sound Off), and the LFO of Voice N (1-4) runs a little faster, by about 0.0007 Hz x (N - 1), so that they drift apart over minutes; the Red Noise is shared. The 4 Synths of PRA32-U2/M are the same: Sub Synth N (1-3) starts at N / 4 and runs faster by about 0.0007 Hz x N, as Voice N + 1 of the Main Synth
    - NOTE: In the Monophonic modes (Voice Mode: Mon, LP, and Lgt), which play Voice 1, VOn, VOf, and VAu work as On, Off, and Aut
- Breath Filter Amt [- to +]: 2x the other Amt parameters (e.g. Filter EG Amt), so that a weak breath also opens the Filter
    - -60 (0-4): -240 (min)
    - +30 (94): +120, the Cutoff +120 with the max breath
    - +60 (124-127): +240 (max), the Cutoff +120 with the half breath
    - Shown: the Cutoff with the max breath, as Filter EG Amt (e.g. "+4", "120", and "240")
- Breath Amp Mode [Off|Q|L|LO|QO|Opn]
    - 0 (0-12): Off, The Amp follows the EG (the Breath Controller does not modulate the Amp)
    - 26 (13-38): Quadratic Curve (Q), The Amp follows the EG, x the Breath Controller (Quadratic Curve)
    - 51 (39-63): Linear Curve (L), The Amp follows the EG, x the Breath Controller (Linear Curve)
    - 77 (64-89): Linear Curve Open (LO), The Amp is held open (the EG is not used), x the Breath Controller (Linear Curve)
    - 102 (90-115): Quadratic Curve Open (QO), The Amp is held open (the EG is not used), x the Breath Controller (Quadratic Curve)
    - 127 (116-127): Open (Opn), The Amp is held open at the max (the EG is not used), and the level is controlled by the Filter (e.g. Breath Filter Amt)
    - NOTE: In the Open modes (LO, QO, Opn), the Amp EG, Amp Level Velo Sens, and the release are not effective, and the voices sound also after the note off (and before the first note on, at C4)
        - All Sound Off does not stop the sound in the Open modes (it works as All Notes Off, without resetting the Osc, the Filter, and the LFO)
        - With LO and QO, the sound stops when the Breath Controller is 0
        - With Opn, the level is controlled by the Low Pass Filter (Filter Mode: LP): close the Filter (a low Cutoff and a low Resonance) to stop the sound, and open it (by Cutoff, Breath Filter Amt, Filter EG Amt, or LFO Filter Amt) to sound
            - NOTE: Low notes may still be heard faintly at the min Cutoff
        - Opn can also be used for drones: the voices keep sounding at the last notes (in the Polyphonic Mode, the last chord of up to 4 notes), and the Filter EG and LFO still work
        - LO and QO are intended for the Monophonic or Legato Voice Mode, as the note-off voices keep sounding in the Polyphonic Mode
- After Touch LFO Amt: The LFO depth added by the max after touch (Polyphonic Key Pressure or Channel Pressure)
    - 0: +0% (min)
    - 64: +50%
    - 127: +100% (max)
- Portamento Mode [ESw|ESw|LSw|LSw|LAu|EAu]: What turns on and off the Portamento, and how the pitch moves
    - 0 (0-12): Exponential Switch (ESw), The Portamento is on only while Portamento Switch (Control Change #65) is On (64-127);
      the pitch approaches the note exponentially, by the time of "Portamento Time"
    - 26 (13-38): Exponential Switch (ESw)
    - 51 (39-63): Linear Switch (LSw), The Portamento is on only while Portamento Switch is On; the pitch moves linearly, at the rate
      of "Portamento Time" (as GM2)
    - 77 (64-89): Linear Switch (LSw)
    - 102 (90-115): Linear Auto (LAu), The Portamento is always on; the pitch moves linearly
    - 127 (116-127): Exponential Auto (EAu), The Portamento is always on; the pitch approaches the note exponentially
    - NOTE: In Legato Portamento (Voice Mode: LP), the Portamento is effective only for the legato notes, also while it is on
      (exponential or linear, by this)
- Osc Saw Wave Mode [Str|Cur]
    - 0 (0-63): Straight Saw Wave
    - 127 (64-127): Curved Saw Wave, This does not affect the Pulse Wave
- Stretch Tune [- to +]: The pitch stretched around note number 60
    - -64 (0): -37.5 cent at note number 108 (+37.5 cent at note number 12) (min)
    - +8 (72): +4.7 cent at note number 108 (-4.7 cent at note number 12)
    - +63 (127): +36.9 cent at note number 108 (-36.9 cent at note number 12) (max)
    - Shown: the cents at note number 108 (e.g. "+5" at +8, and "-38" at -64)
- Pan: Constant power panning
    - -64 (0-1): Left (L +3.0 dB, R Off) (min)
    - +0 (64): Center (L 0 dB, R 0 dB)
    - +63 (127): Right (L Off, R +3.0 dB) (max)
    - Shown: "L63" (0 and 1), ..., "C" (64), ..., "R63" (127)
- Assign Mode [Jmp|Cat|Rel]: How General Purpose Controller 1-4 (Control Change #16-19) control the parameters of Assign #16-#19 (see Performance Controllers)
    - 0 (0-31): Jump (Jmp), At once, as the Control Change of the number (the parameter jumps to the value)
    - 64 (32-95): Catch (Cat), From when the value comes to that of the parameter (the same, or across it), as the VRs of the panel of PRA32-U2/P,
      so that the parameter does not jump (e.g. after a Program Change, or the parameter changed otherwise); the parameter then follows it
    - 127 (96-127): Relative (Rel), The value - 64 added to that of the parameter (e.g. +1 by 65, -1 by 63), within 0-127, for an endless encoder
      that sends them; the sliders of PRA32-U2 Editor and the knobs of the panel (Performance Controls, and Pages B-24 and B-25) work as Jmp
- Assign #16-#19: The parameter that General Purpose Controller 1-4 (Control Change #16-19) control, by its Control Change number (see Performance Controllers)
    - 0, 16-19 (General Purpose Controller 1-4), 48-51 (Assign #16-#19), 64 (Sustain Pedal), 80 (Voice Group Mode), 106 (Write Parameters to Program), 111-119 (Randomize Synth and Program Change by CC), and 120-127 (the Channel Mode Messages): Off
    - e.g. 74: Filter Cutoff, 71: Filter Resonance, 47: Osc 1 Shape, 20: Osc 1 Morph, and 1: Modulation Wheel
    - Shown: the number, or "Off"
- Distortion Drive: The drive into the Distortion FX, 2^(value / 24) (6 dB every 24 values, as Filter Drive), for the synths whose FX Routing is Dst; the output is lowered by 15/16 of the drive in dB up to 96 (x 16), and by as much as there above it, where most of a sound is past the shoulder already, so that the level stays within about 2 dB as the drive goes up (a single quiet note gets a few dB louder at the top). The shoulder below zero is shorter than above (even harmonics too), the aliasing is reduced by ADAA (antiderivative anti-aliasing), and a tone (a 1-pole low pass) after it takes the edge off the harmonics, more with more drive (none at 0, about 10 kHz at 64, 5 kHz at 127)
    - 0: x 1.00 (0 dB, min: a sound below full scale passes as it is)
    - 48: x 4.00 (+12 dB)
    - 127: x 39.2 (+31.9 dB, max)
    - Shown: the dB (e.g. "+6", and "+32")
    - NOTE: On a single note of the Saw Wave, which has every harmonic already, the Distortion changes the tone little; it is heard best on a chord (from a Distortion Drive of about 30 up), with a high Filter Resonance (about 110 or above, from a Distortion Drive of about 72 up), or with the Sine or the Triangle Wave
    - NOTE: Changed by "Randomize FX" (of PRA32-U2 Editor and PRA32-U2 Native), not by "Randomize Synth"
- FX Routing [Dst|Cho|Dly|Dly|Cmp|Dry]: Where the output is input to the FX (connected in series: Distortion -> Chorus -> Delay -> Output Compressor)
    - 0 (0-12): Dst (through the Distortion, the Chorus, the Delay, and the Output Compressor)
    - 26 (13-38): Chorus (through the Chorus, the Delay, and the Output Compressor, not the Distortion)
    - 51 (39-63): Delay (through the Delay and the Output Compressor)
    - 77 (64-89): Delay
    - 102 (90-115): Comp (through the Output Compressor only)
    - 127 (116-127): Dry (through none of them)
        - The output is still delayed by 1 ms and lowered by 2.5 dB (x 0.75) as the others, and is clipped (soft clipping) if it is too loud
    - In PRA32-U2/M, each synth has its own FX Routing, while the other FX parameters of the Main Synth apply to all synths
- Chorus Level: The level of the chorused signal L (0.0 at 0 to 1.0 at 127, 2 values a step) added to the input; the sum is lowered by about 1 / sqrt(1 + L^2) (x 0.707 at 127, where the two are equal), so that the level stays within about 1 dB as the Chorus Level goes up
    - Shown: L in % (e.g. "50%" at 63-64, and "100")
- Chorus Rate: The frequency of the LFO
    - 0: 0.0114 Hz (min)
    - 64: 0.482 Hz
    - 127: 18.4 Hz (max)
    - Shown: the frequency (e.g. ".01", ".48", and "18")
- Chorus Depth: The swing of the delay time, value / 24 ms
    - 0: +/- 0 ms (min)
    - 64: +/- 2.67 ms
    - 126 (126-127): +/- 5.25 ms (max)
    - Shown: the milliseconds, as the frequency (e.g. ".04" at 1, "2.7", and "5.3")
- Delay Level: Delay Send Level, 2 values a step
    - Shown: the % (e.g. "50%" at 63-64, and "100")
- Delay Mode [Ste|PP|Rev]
    - 0 (0-31): Stereo Delay
    - 64 (32-95): Ping Pong Delay
    - 127 (96-127): Reverb
        - The Delay Level, Delay Time, and Delay Feedback work as they do for the Delay: the same values give about the same length and loudness of the tail
        - Delay Time sets the size (the first reflection comes at about 1/3 of the Delay Time)
        - Delay Feedback sets the decay (the tail falls by the Delay Feedback over each 1.5 x Delay Time, so that it sounds about as long as the Delay's echoes)
        - When the Delay Time is changed, the size follows it slowly (within +/-3% of the pitch)
- Delay Time
    - 0: 1 ms (min)
    - 42: 100 ms
    - 64: 173.3 ms
    - 114: 340 ms (max)
    - The eighth note time: 42 (300 BPM), 57 (200 BPM), 62 (180 BPM), 72 (150 BPM), 87 (120 BPM), and 102 (100 BPM)
    - Shown: the milliseconds (e.g. "100", and "173")
    - NOTE: When the Delay Time is changed, the delay time follows it within +/-25% of the pitch, as on a tape delay
- Delay Feedback: 0% (0, min) to 49.6% (127, max), value / 256
    - Shown: the % (e.g. "25%" at 64, and "49%" at 127)

## Performance Controllers

- Not stored in the programs; the values at startup and by Reset All Controllers are shown as "Reset"
- Pitch Bend: Bends the pitch by "Pitch Bend Range" (Reset: center)
- Modulation Wheel (Control Change #1): Adds to the LFO depth (127: +100%) (Reset: 0)
- Breath Controller (Control Change #2): Modulates the Filter Cutoff by "Breath Filter Amt", and the Amp by "Breath Amp Mode" (Reset: 0)
- Expression (Control Change #11): The level, in a quadratic curve (64: 25%, about -12 dB) (Reset: 127)
- Sustain Pedal [Off|On] (Control Change #64): While it is On (64-127), the note offs are held, and they are released when it is turned Off (Reset: Off)
    - It is also turned Off by All Notes Off, All Sound Off, Omni Mode Off/On, and Mono/Poly Mode On
- Portamento Switch [Off|On] (Control Change #65): While it is On (64-127), the Portamento is on, if "Portamento Mode" is ESw or LSw (Reset: Off)
- After Touch (Polyphonic Key Pressure and Channel Pressure): Adds to the LFO depth by "After Touch LFO Amt" (Reset: 0)
- General Purpose Controller 1-4 (Control Change #16-19): Work as the Control Change of the number that "Assign #16-#19" set (e.g. a knob of a keyboard for
  a parameter of each program), at once (Assign Mode Jmp), from when the value comes to that of the parameter (Cat), or as relative values (Rel)
    - With Assign Mode Jmp and Assign #16-#19 set to 44-47, they work as EG Att/Dec Key Track, LFO Depth, Voice Mode, and Osc 1 Shape did on them before v3.22.0
    - PRA32-U2/M: On a channel of a group (e.g. the Round Robin) or the Layering, each synth takes it by its own Assign Mode, Assign #16-#19,
      and value of the parameter: with Assign Mode Cat, the synths whose values the knob comes to first follow it first

## Parameter Smoothing

- The parameters are smoothed, so that their steps (e.g. sent by MIDI controllers every 10 ms) are not heard
- The smoothing is updated at 6 kHz (every other control period of 12 kHz), for the CPU usage
- The EG and LFO modulations themselves are not smoothed (not delayed)
- 2 stages (5.3 ms average delay, 99% in 18 ms), which round off the corners of the steps, for the amounts and the balances below
- The amounts (of the tone, the level, or a modulation), whose steps are easily heard
    - Filter Cutoff, Filter Resonance, Filter EG Amt, LFO Filter Amt, Breath Filter Amt, Filter Drive, and the gains of the Filter Mode
    - Osc 1 Shape
    - EG Mod Amt and LFO Mod Amt, when EG Mod Dst and LFO Mod Dst are F (Filter Cutoff) or 1S (Osc 1 Shape)
    - Amp Gain
    - LFO Depth (with LFO Fade Time, Modulation Wheel, and After Touch)
    - Distortion Drive, Chorus Level, Chorus Depth, Delay Level, Delay Feedback
- The balances (between two sounds, or left and right), which keep the total amount about the same
    - Osc 1 Morph, Mixer Osc Mix, Mixer Noise/Sub Osc, Pan
    - For the Sine Wave and the Wave Tables, the smoothed Osc 1 Morph is rounded to the controller value, as it switches the ratio or the table step by step
- 1 stage (1.2 ms time constant, 95% in 3.7 ms), for the performance controllers, whose attack must not be softened, but whose steps must not click
    - Breath Controller (x Breath Filter Amt)
    - Expression x Breath Controller (Breath Amp Mode)
- 1 stage, for the Delay Time (5.3 ms), which moves in its own way
    - The Delay Time is also slew-limited, bending the pitch of the echoes (within +/-25%), as on a tape delay
- The smoothed parameters are calculated in fine steps (not in the controller value steps)
- Not smoothed: the pitch parameters, so that the pitch follows right away (Pitch Bend, and EG Mod Amt and LFO Mod Amt when EG Mod Dst and LFO Mod Dst are P or 2P), and the parameters whose steps are part of the sound (e.g. Osc 2 Coarse/Pitch, LFO Rate, EG times)
- See the comments in "pra32-u2-common.h" for details
